### The freshness principle transfers to artificial delayed-feedback controllers

If the necessity structure is a property of delayed feedback control rather than of this particular plant, it should appear in non-biological tasks where a delayed channel carries the full state and reference. We preregistered three standard benchmarks (cart-pole, rotary pendulum, and cart-pole with a moving reference), each with 30 randomized plants, an 8-point delay grid from 0 to 600 ms, process noise, and six controller families: classical LQR on the delayed observation (C), velocity extrapolation from the delayed sample (E1), a stochastic forward-model observer whose propagation window spans the delay (E2, with an exact and a 10% parameter-mismatch version), fresh-state LQR with only the reference delayed (F), and fresh-state LQR with the delayed-state term additionally stacked in, ungated (F+) (Fig. 23; preregistration SHA-256 6e56c187).

The predictions made from the model layer were borne out (Fig. 23; C53-C55). **F was stable at every delay through 600 ms in all three task families (0% failure at every grid point), while C and E1 crossed 50% failure between 50 and 200 ms.** The delay-embedding observer improved on the naive baselines (critical delays of 150-300 ms) but never approached the fresh-state family, and its 10% mismatch variant did not consistently help (150-300 ms). Stacking the delayed-state term on top of fresh-state feedback — the ungated architecture — degraded the fresh family sharply, from fully stable to critical delays of 150-400 ms (C54), reproducing, on three non-biological tasks, the conditional result from the steering model: the delayed self-state pathway must be suppressed, not summed (C50). The zero-delay identity between C and F was verified in self-tests; all failure curves include a fixed mid-episode state impulse, and the preregistered secondary metric (recovery time among survivors) is reported in the summary artifacts (C55).

Two scope statements bound this transfer. The tasks are linearizations with Gaussian process noise; a delay-embedding observer in a noiseless deterministic setting can in principle reconstruct the state exactly, and our result establishes the practical claim under noise and mismatch, not an impossibility result. And the fresh-state family is privileged by construction — it is the artificial analog of proprioception versus delayed vision — so what transfers is the **necessity structure** (fresh state suffices; delayed-only estimation degrades with delay; stacking the delayed pathway without suppression hurts), not any particular controller. Within those bounds, this is the demonstration the model layer alone could not provide: the information hierarchy is a property of delayed feedback control, not of the fly.
